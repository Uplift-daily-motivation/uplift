Introduction

Welcome to Uplift ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience on our app. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our Uplift mobile application.

By using Uplift, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our app.

Information We Collect

Personal Information

We may collect the following personal information that you voluntarily provide:

  • Profile Information: Name, zodiac sign, religious preferences, relationship status, employment status
  • Preferences: Favorite quotes, categories, notification settings
  • Usage Data: App usage patterns, features accessed, time spent in app
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, app version

Automatically Collected Information

We automatically collect certain information when you use our app:

  • Analytics Data: App performance, crash reports, usage statistics
  • Device Identifiers: Device ID, advertising identifier (if applicable)
  • Log Data: IP address, browser type, access times, pages viewed

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Personalization: Provide personalized quotes and content based on your preferences
  • App Functionality: Enable core app features like favorites, streaks, and notifications
  • Improvement: Analyze usage patterns to improve app performance and user experience
  • Communication: Send notifications and updates about the app
  • Support: Provide customer support and respond to inquiries
  • Security: Protect against fraud and ensure app security

AI and Third-Party Services

OpenAI Integration

Our app uses OpenAI's API to generate personalized quotes and affirmations. When you use AI-generated content:

  • Your preferences (zodiac sign, religion, etc.) are sent to OpenAI to generate relevant content
  • We do not send personal identifying information to OpenAI
  • OpenAI's privacy policy applies to data processed by their services
  • You can opt out of AI-generated content in app settings

Other Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services for:

  • Analytics: App usage analytics and performance monitoring
  • Payment Processing: Secure subscription payments through Apple's StoreKit
  • Cloud Storage: Data synchronization across devices

Data Storage and Security

Local Storage

Most of your data is stored locally on your device using:

  • UserDefaults for app preferences and settings
  • Core Data for favorites and quote history
  • Keychain for secure storage of sensitive information

Cloud Storage

Premium users can optionally sync data using Apple's CloudKit:

  • Favorites and preferences are synced across devices
  • Data is encrypted in transit and at rest
  • You can disable cloud sync at any time

Security Measures

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information:

  • Encryption of sensitive data
  • Secure API communications
  • Regular security updates
  • Access controls and authentication
