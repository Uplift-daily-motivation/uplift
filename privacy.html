Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 October 2026

Uplift: Daily Motivation (“Uplift”, “the app”) is made by Hokfam Ltd (“we”, “us”). We built Uplift to be private by default. This policy explains, in plain language, what the app does with information.

In short: there is no account and no sign‑up. What you enter in Uplift stays on your device. We do not run analytics, advertising or tracking in the app, we do not sell data, and we do not receive your quotes, favourites or onboarding answers.

Information stored on your device

To personalise your quotes, Uplift saves the following locally on your iPhone or iPad (in the app’s own storage and in a shared container used only by Uplift’s widgets):

  • the name you choose to be greeted by (optional);
  • your onboarding answers, such as relationship, work, faith and focus areas (all optional);
  • your favourites, chosen theme, reminder times and other settings;
  • simple in‑app counters, such as your daily streak and how many quotes you have read, used to show your streak and to decide when to ask if you would like to rate the app.

This information is not sent to us. It may be included in your device’s own iCloud or computer backups, which are managed by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy. Deleting the app deletes this data from the device. You can change your profile at any time in Settings.

Notifications

Daily reminders are local notifications scheduled by iOS on your device. We do not use a push‑notification server and do not receive your reminder times. You can turn reminders off in the app or in iOS Settings › Notifications › Uplift.

Purchases

Uplift Premium is sold through Apple’s App Store using StoreKit. Apple processes your payment; we never see your card or payment details. The app checks your subscription status with Apple on the device. Apple may share limited, non‑identifying sales information with us through App Store Connect, as described in Apple’s own privacy policy.

Analytics, advertising and AI

The app contains no third‑party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs and does not use the Advertising Identifier. Quotes come from the library built into the app. The current version does not send your information to any artificial‑intelligence service. If that ever changes, we will update this policy and ask for your consent first where required.

Ratings and app reviews

Uplift may use Apple’s standard in‑app rating prompt. Any rating or review you leave is handled by Apple.

Children

Uplift is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Your rights

Because we do not hold personal data about you, there is usually nothing for us to access, correct or delete; you control everything on your device. If you live in the UK or EEA you still have rights under data‑protection law, and you can contact us or your local supervisory authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner’s Office).

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, analytics or tracking scripts. GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses for security, as described in GitHub’s privacy statement.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, mention it in the app’s release notes.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@uplift-app.com
