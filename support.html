Support

We read every message and usually reply within a few working days.

Contact us

Email support@uplift-app.com. It helps to include your device model, iOS version and the Uplift version (shown on the App Store page or in Settings › General › iPhone Storage › Uplift).

Email support

Reminders aren’t arriving

  • Check that a daily reminder is switched on in Uplift › Settings and the time is right.
  • In iOS Settings › Notifications › Uplift, make sure Allow Notifications is on.
  • Focus modes (such as Sleep or Do Not Disturb) can silence notifications. Add Uplift as an allowed app if you want reminders during a Focus.

Adding a widget

Touch and hold an empty area of the Home Screen, tap Edit › Add Widget, search for Uplift and choose a size. For the Lock Screen, touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customise › Lock Screen, then tap the widget area. Widgets use your current theme; if one looks out of date, open Uplift once to refresh it.

Focus mode

On the Today screen, the controls fade after three seconds so only the quote and author remain. Tap the screen to bring them back.

Changing your profile or focus areas

Open Settings › You to edit your name and the answers you gave during onboarding. Your Today feed updates to match.

Uplift Premium

  • Restore purchases: open Uplift › Settings › Restore purchases while signed in with the Apple Account you used to subscribe.
  • Cancel or change plan: iOS Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Uplift. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal to avoid the next charge.
  • Refunds: purchases are processed by Apple; request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

Uplift has no account. Your favourites and settings live on your device, so deleting the app removes them. See the Privacy Policy for details.
