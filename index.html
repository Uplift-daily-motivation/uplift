Uplift: Daily Motivation

One thoughtful quote every day, chosen around what matters to you. Set in beautiful photo themes, on your Home Screen and at the time you choose.

Download on theApp Store See it in action

Free to download for iPhone and iPad. Optional Uplift Premium subscription.

Pick what matters to you: calm, self‑love, gratitude, work, courage, faith. Uplift leans your day that way, one good thought at a time.

Inside the app

Calm by design.
Yours in every detail.

01 — TODAY

Your quote of the day.

Open Uplift to a quote picked for your focus areas, then swipe up whenever you want another. Save it, share it as an image on your theme, or simply sit with it.

02 — EXPLORE

Categories for what matters.

Browse self‑love, calm, gratitude, courage, focus, healing and many more. Each category is its own collection, written by writers, thinkers and leaders.

03 — FAVOURITES

Keep the ones that land.

Tap the heart to save a quote. Your favourites are searchable, easy to copy and share, and stay on your device.

04 — THEMES

A photo theme on every screen.

Choose the backdrop that suits your mood. Your theme follows you through Today, Explore, Favourites, Settings and your widgets, with light and dark looks too.

Personalised onboarding

It starts by getting to know you.

A few quick, optional questions about your relationships, your work, whether faith is part of your story and what you’d like more of. Uplift uses your answers to choose quotes that fit your life right now. Change them any time in Settings.

Uplift Premium

More of what helps, whenever you need it.

Uplift is free to download. Premium is optional and unlocks:

  • Every category, fully openThe complete library in every category.
  • Reminders through the dayGentle nudges at the times you choose.
  • Every themeAll photo themes for the app and your widgets.
  • Support an independent appHelp keep Uplift calm and ad‑free.

Uplift Premium is an auto‑renewing subscription, billed monthly or yearly. Prices are shown in the app in your local currency before you buy. Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase. The subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the period ends. Manage or cancel any time in your Apple Account settings. See the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.
